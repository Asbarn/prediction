## Conclusion

Milestone v1.0 delivers the complete prediction market arbitrage signal pipeline as designed. All 9 phases are implemented with 30 plans, 411+ tests, and 97 verified observable truths. The system can:

1. Connect to Deribit, Polymarket, and Kalshi WebSocket feeds with auto-reconnection
2. Normalize order books to unified MarketSnapshot format
3. Map equivalent instruments across venues with basis risk scoring
4. Detect 4-pattern prediction market spreads with fee-adjusted costs
5. Extract options-implied probabilities via IV solving and call spread replication
6. Generate cross-asset arbitrage signals with dynamic thresholds
7. Track paper trade P&L with daily rollups
8. Export metrics to Prometheus and structured logs to JSONL
9. Replay historical recordings through the full pipeline
10. Report system health via HTTP endpoint

No critical blockers prevent milestone completion. The accumulated tech debt (10 items) is non-blocking and can be addressed in the next milestone or tracked in backlog.
