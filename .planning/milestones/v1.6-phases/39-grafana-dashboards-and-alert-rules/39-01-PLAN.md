1. **Create `grafana/provisioning/alerting/rules.yml`** (MON-08) -- Three alert rules in a single group:

   **Alert 1: Feed Down** -- Fires when any venue's feed_available is < 1 for 5 minutes.
   - PromQL (refId A): `feed_available` (instant query)
   - Threshold (refId B): `__expr__` threshold, expression referencing A, evaluator `lt` with param 1, reducer `last`
   - `for: 5m`, `noDataState: Alerting`, `execErrState: Alerting`
   - Annotations: summary "Feed {{ $labels.venue }} is DOWN"
   - Labels: severity: critical

   **Alert 2: Zero Spread Computations** -- Fires when no spread computations for 30 minutes.
   - PromQL (refId A): `rate(spread_computations_total[5m])` (instant query)
   - Threshold (refId B): `__expr__` threshold, expression referencing A, evaluator `lt` with param 0.001 (effectively zero, avoids float comparison issues)
   - `for: 30m`, `noDataState: Alerting`, `execErrState: Alerting`
   - Annotations: summary "No spread computations for 30 minutes"
   - Labels: severity: warning

   **Alert 3: High Staleness Rejection Rate** -- Fires when staleness rejection ratio exceeds 50%.
   - PromQL (refId A): `rate(arb_staleness_rejections[5m]) / rate(arb_computations_total[5m])` (instant query)
   - Threshold (refId B): `__expr__` threshold, expression referencing A, evaluator `gt` with param 0.5
   - `for: 5m`, `noDataState: OK` (no data means no computations, not a problem), `execErrState: Alerting`
   - Annotations: summary "Staleness rejection rate exceeds 50%"
   - Labels: severity: warning

   Use the exact YAML structure from the research (Pattern 3 and full code example). Group name: "prediction-critical", folder: "Prediction Alerts", interval: 1m.
